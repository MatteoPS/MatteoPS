# Matteo Perini

Computational biologist at Columbia University (Climate School, [Shaman Group](https://blogs.cuit.columbia.edu/jls106/)), based in New York City.

- 🔬 I build mathematical and statistical models of infectious disease and public health data, from epidemic forecasting to spatiotemporal surveillance
- 🧬 Previously pathogen genomics and metagenomics (PhD, University of Milan, 2022)
- 🤖 Exploring LLM agents for my own field, e.g. [diagnosing forecasting pipelines](https://github.com/MatteoPS/eakf-agentic-diagnostics)
- 🏃 Training for my second NYC Marathon with [agentic-runplan](https://github.com/MatteoPS/agentic-runplan), a Claude Code–built assistant that adjusts workouts and fueling as training unfolds
